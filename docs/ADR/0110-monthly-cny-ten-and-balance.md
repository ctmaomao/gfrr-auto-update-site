# ADR-0110: Monthly CNY10 cap and exact CNY balance admission

## Status and scope

2026-11-12 owner requests a combined CNY10 monthly limit for Macro Risk and Bubble Watch, with Tavily and Brave remaining free-only. Local implementation based on main `a9f6cab687a60ec3b79897fe9025a541740f0dd1`; independent exact-tree review and checked serial publication remain required. This change performs no remote configuration, balance/search/provider request, ledger mutation, production data edit or activation. Code publication and account activation are separate outcomes.

## Decision

The monthly policy becomes `deepseek-flash-cny-20261112-v2`, cap `10000000` micro-CNY, and schema `deepseek-budget-v2`. The branch and credential/variable interfaces are unchanged. The offline preparer creates only a v2 candidate. It cannot initialize remotely; creation must separately recheck that the branch is absent and use create-only genesis semantics. Any existing monthly v1 ledger fails closed without writes. No automatic v1 upgrade, ref replacement, deletion, schema rewriting or fallback exists; a discovered v1 ledger needs a separate reviewed append-only migration design.

The prior complete policy object is frozen as `LEGACY_POLICY`, with digest `e5d89e09ba8bb0906bf0efeded3d185087809c9bf56aff44e048b083afee7518`. Both existing one-shot grants explicitly retain this object/digest and validate their historical price reviews against it. Their branches, grants, expiry, reserve/outcome history, permanent consumed slots and approved grant/price digests remain unchanged. A monthly v2 price approval cannot validate a legacy grant review, and a legacy price review cannot initialize monthly v2. No existing remote ledger is edited by this patch.

Monthly v2 policy digest is `32bd439d1bf9f07f34cd7318373daf1d8aa5fbd3653841a384b2545d481ddba7`. Each attempt still reserves the full CNY2.161152, so zero historical debt allows at most four attempts (CNY8.644608) across both consumers/all keys/modes in 31 days, never a fifth (CNY10.805760). Lower token receipts never refund or reduce the reservation. Successful monthly requests retain full reserve through completion +31 days +152 seconds, including equality; uncertain attempts never age away. Price reviews remain at most24 hours with the76-second expiry margin; no retry, request/response/usage, clock/CAS and fact/source/quality protections change.

Unknown historical spend uses a full CNY10 quarantine. Other existing bootstrap evidence modes remain strict and independently approved. The two completed one-shot rounds contain four permanent reservations totaling CNY8.644608; these are not zero-spend evidence, cannot be reset/refunded or reused, and do not establish complete all-key history. An activation reviewer must reconcile the full historical scope, including these attempts, without changing their permanent legacy records. A balance, token estimate or small bill display is not adequate history proof. Missing evidence leaves activation held; this patch does not promise immediate new articles.

The monthly balance validator now uses precisely the former one-shot BigInt contract, and one-shot exports an alias to that shared validator. It requires availability=true, exactly one CNY row, optionally one USD row whose three amounts are exactly zero, and no duplicate/unknown currencies or extra fields. Every amount is a nonnegative decimal string of at most32 characters and at most8 fractional digits. Exact total equals granted plus topped-up; CNY total covers the full CNY2.161152 reserve. Nonzero USD, malformed/inconsistent amounts or insufficient CNY hold before reservation/POST. No FX, USD spend, inferred debit priority, amount logging or separate diagnostic probe is added.

Tavily/Brave free-only gates and all quotas/history remain unchanged by this monthly patch. Brave evidence/record repair, if performed, is a distinct operation and must not clear unresolved requests or fabricate free allowance. No secret entry point, dependency, frontend, workflow or production data changes are included.

## Explicit checker changes and acceptance

This ADR narrowly supersedes ADR-0090's v1 CNY5/two-attempt assertions and ADR-0097's monthly exclusion. `deepseek-budget.test.mjs` changes cap fixtures to CNY10, tests four admitted attempts and fifth refusal (including both consumers, rotating keys, shared modes, last-slot races and opposite-clock month-end boundaries), retains all old hold/history/expiry tests, and strengthens balance fixtures to require actual sufficient funding. The complete-history fixture now allows three requests against a CNY2 debit and rejects the fourth; the dashboard CNY0.15 fixture allows four and rejects the fifth.

One-shot tests explicitly import the frozen legacy policy. Captured baseline policy and both grant digests are literal regressions; tests continue to exercise consumed old ledger structures, append-only transitions and independent grant selection. The old assertion rejecting a zero USD monthly row is intentionally replaced by shared-reader acceptance, with new monthly pre-reservation negatives for nonzero USD, inconsistent/insufficient CNY, duplicate/unknown rows and malformed amounts. New tests reject v1 monthly schema and legacy price approval without any write or balance read. `macro-budget-skip.test.mjs` updates its eligible monthly fixture to v2/CNY10 and explicitly requires old v1 to stay red, never expected-skip. No assertions are skipped, no ignore list is added, and no factual/editorial quality checker is relaxed.

Required verification: focused offline budget suites, `npm run check:changed` selecting and executing full `check:all`, exact diff independent review, then coordinator-controlled serial publication. Test success is not remote history verification, price approval or initialization. See [operations](../OPERATIONS.md#shared-editorial-budget-and-free-search-admission) for activation prerequisites.
