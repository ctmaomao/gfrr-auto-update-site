# ADR-0044: Contemporaneous pressure research and prospective shadow

Status: Owner authorized the seven-risk remediation on 2026-09-11 and selected contemporaneous market/macro pressure as the main objective, with forecasts and position sizing evaluated separately. Research implementation and remote integration are authorized. Production model replacement remains evidence-gated; historical comparisons below did not establish a universally better replacement.

## Context and objective

The [system audit](../SCORE_SYSTEM_AUDIT_2026_09_11.md) found discontinuous tail floors, duplicated factors, fixed price scales, ON RRP misinterpretation and unsupported probability/portfolio claims. A numerically lower candidate is not evidence of better measurement. We need a reproducible experiment and actual subsequent observations, not a retrospectively reconstructed shadow period.

Primary objective: contemporaneous market and macro pressure transmitted through US-market-based proxies. This is not complete worldwide financial-system coverage, a crisis probability, a market-top prediction or an optimized portfolio recommendation. Future crisis forecasting and position sizing have separate targets and validation requirements; this experiment supplies neither.

## Decision

The fixed protocol is [pressure-model-research.json](../../config/pressure-model-research.json). Seven primitive series enter six disjoint channels: BAA10Y credit spread, log VIX, SPX trailing-year drawdown, absolute four-week Treasury yield change, positive four-week broad-dollar log change, and an energy/inflation channel averaging separately scaled oil log-change magnitude and breakeven level. BAA10Y is explicitly its own credit measure, not a renamed HY OAS. ON RRP is excluded from candidate scoring; it does not become bank reserves. World Order, transport contributions, external AI and probability-like scenario fields do not enter this candidate.

Only strictly earlier Friday samples calibrate each score. Require at least 104 genuine weeks; compare windows of 104/156/260 weeks. Scale each feature using prior median and robust dispersion (MAD/IQR with documented positive numerical floors), then apply a smooth arctangent map. This avoids step-function floors and fixed absolute price caps. A flat training window is disclosed through scale-floor diagnostics. It does not prove that the chosen transformations cover every kind of stress.

The seven prespecified variants compare equal weighting, continuous mean/RMS tail blends, and inverse-positive-correlation redundancy adjustment. The latter is an experimental alternative: unique input ownership is not economic independence, and actual widespread stress may be correlated. Weight concentration and correlation participation are reported separately. Each input's single ownership, exact transformation and source-age assumptions are validated against the implementation. No parameter search selects a winner using today's score.

Historical comparison uses the same available dates for the current production formula and candidates, with explicit legacy proxies and missing coverage. STLFSI4 and NFCI are convergent reference indices, not independent ground truth; they share market inputs and differ in scope. Report rank correlation, AUROC, confusion counts under a predeclared 70-point research alert and 2:1 false-negative/false-positive cost, annual results and 13-week block-bootstrap uncertainty. This alert/cost is an experimental convention, not calibrated investor utility. Test extra publication lag and parameter sensitivity. Do not compute a Brier score or label the score as probability; future-event lead time is not applicable to this nowcast objective.

Public historical CSV is latest-vintage data with explicit assumed release lags, not real-time release history. This does not become point-in-time evidence merely by removing future observation dates. ALFRED defines vintages as data actually available on a past date; no such complete input-vintage panel has been recovered here. Historical data-source availability prevents a like-for-like candidate replay of 2008. All these exclusions remain visible in the report.

## Forward evidence, automation and publication

Run `Pressure Model Research Shadow` on main at 01:15 UTC daily. It uses read-only repository/actions permissions and at most fifteen bounded public FRED CSV requests per run, without credentials for paid sources, retries, production writes, deploy commands or scoring imports from the candidate into Daily. No raw history cache is uploaded: only the derived report and cumulative ledger are retained as Actions artifacts. Failure diagnostics have a separate artifact name so they cannot replace the last usable ledger.

The cohort is keyed by both protocol and normalized implementation-source hashes, including the legacy baseline formula and rules. Store at most one actual runtime observation per UTC date: source observations, change references, equity-high summary, derived features, all variant scores and coefficients, and the lag-matched legacy public-history replay. Validate restored timestamps, source-age assumptions, references, feature reconstruction, candidate score replay and hash chains, including offline reads. A rerun cannot overwrite an earlier score, manufacture independent inputs through coefficient updates or backfill elapsed time. Implementation/protocol changes start a distinct cohort. These hashes detect accidental modification; they are not a cryptographic certification of an external source's truth or a complete historical-vintage database. Artifact provenance and independent review remain necessary.

The observational gates require 84 elapsed days, 40 distinct input snapshots and 12 matched benchmark weeks. They are minimum collection requirements, not proof of predictive accuracy or sufficient statistical power. `eligibleForProduction` remains false until a separate independent model review accepts the outcome definition, measurement validity, revision/lag sensitivity, missing-data behavior and migration plan. Historical results alone cannot promote a candidate. There is no automatic path into `deriveRisk`, six production modules, execution locks or portfolio instructions. Broad implementation authorization does not justify marking unmet evidence gates as passed.

## Initial evidence and consequences

See the [research report](../PRESSURE_MODEL_RESEARCH_2026_09_11.md). The final comparison applies identical assumed publication lags to shared inputs in both models, retains an unlagged legacy diagnostic separately and freezes the retrospective cutoff at 2026-09-11. On 359 matched benchmark weeks, the simple equal-weight model's STLFSI rank correlation was approximately 0.642 versus -0.050 for the lag-matched existing formula; with NFCI it was 0.225 versus 0.605. Correlation adjustment did not consistently improve the simpler model. Shared inputs, only eight NFCI-positive weeks and legacy proxies prevent a claim of superiority from these figures. Lag assumptions are not verified release vintages.

Therefore launch the fixed prospective comparison and retain the current production score for now. The earlier implementation hardening can be integrated independently of model promotion. A future production transition must version the model and history and explain that cross-version score differences are not day-to-day market changes. No historical scores are rewritten to make the candidate look better.
