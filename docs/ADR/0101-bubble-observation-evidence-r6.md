## Decision

1. **Preserve observations, distinguish reviews.** `source-observation.mjs` synchronizes only valid, non-future, non-regressing indicator `as_of` dates. Weekly `as_of_date` is stored separately as `reviewAsOfDate` / `reportAsOfDate`; it cannot renew freshness. ARR, VC and enterprise require registered typed bases. Upstream prose, colors or a claimed `current` flag do not supply semantic proof. Upstream typed records also cannot replace the reviewed configuration: same-source dates/values and a new quarter require an explicit reviewed config update; existing live collectors are separate. Other indicators keep their registered semantics and now also use actual indicator dates rather than the report date. A later-dated but invalid local record is not silently overwritten by an older record; this initial explicit repair corrects only the three audited entries.
2. **ARR loses current eligibility, not its history.** Keep the existing 45-calendar-day gate, four reviewed SaaStr posts, explicit Anthropic run-rate binding, amount/interval limits and slope thresholds. The July 31 historical anchors are 102 days old on November 10. The rejected automatic candidate's latest May 28 milestone is 166 days old. r6 registers only a replayable `arr-observation-eligibility-v1` gap: current `as_of=null`, `status=unknown`, `currentEligible=false`; both stale dates and ages remain in the proof. `stale=false` at card level describes the absence of a current dated observation, not freshness of either underlying historical series.
3. **Bind unavailable evidence to actual sources.** The historical proof includes the full reviewed 9/30/65B anchor identities, source URLs, original dates and approximate/lower-bound qualifiers. These anchors are audit-only, not a newly admitted automatic source. A future live stale rejection must carry the actual four parsed SaaStr milestones and their fixed source IDs. The one-time repair uses the exact original published rejection, source issue and file hash. The migration also verifies the complete canonical baseline and history digests before producing output. This legacy rejection proof is valid only for the November 10 issue; it cannot invent a later fetch or renew a checking date. The compressed public-data fixture is that original quarantine snapshot and history, not invented current evidence.
4. **Use one coherent enterprise metric.** r6 registers KPMG significant workforce AI adoption, comparing consecutive quarters for the same question/population. 44% minus 23% is +21 percentage points. Thresholds are change ≥3pp green, −3pp ≤change<3pp yellow, change<−3pp red. This is an explicit semantic migration; it does not reuse the old deployment-level threshold. Keep the original 120-day gate, US $1B+ organization population, 314-executive sample, survey window July 24–August 25 and September 24 publication. The source is `reviewed_curated`; Google/Deloitte/McKinsey remain context only, and the unrelated Wind deployment fallback is not executed for this r6 slot. Future untyped changes hold.
5. **Keep Core-23 and its mathematics.** No slot, weight, primary threshold, category resonance or two-axis upgrade changes. Unknown is neither green nor zero and does not shrink the denominator. Current counts become 5 red / 8 yellow / 7 green / 3 unknown. Primary bounds become 21.7–34.8%; pressure 39.1–52.2%; stage remains 45–55; trigger becomes 34.6–50. Both effective-tier bounds remain high-risk alert. A lower lower-bound is evidence withdrawal, not improved market risk. Old r1–r5 epochs remain readable and unmodified; r6 appends its own history and starts an empty comparable point series.
6. **Historical similarity is not a prediction validation.** Unknown statuses must not enter even the descriptive similarity denominator. The current r6 comparison excludes uncalibrated ARR/enterprise definitions and NVIDIA's changed comparable-flow definition, in addition to the four prior migrations. It remains a fixed hypothetical structure comparison, not an out-of-sample backtest or probability. No historical publication is recomputed.
7. **No broad checker exemption.** Extend the explicit model ceiling to r6, register the one named ARR gap, and replay its identity/date proof. Add strict KPMG public-card and compact-score proof validation. Source audit can warn only for a matching, proven stale ARR rejection or the complete current KPMG reviewed record. HTTP/content/malformed/mismatched failures still fail. Legacy r1–r5 assertions remain; the old r5 unit replay now uses its frozen snapshot rather than a moving production file. No ignore or skip is added.
8. **Preserve the four-source refresh boundary.** r6 remains limited to the existing four collectors. A carried r6 gap or other proof that no longer qualifies at today's clock stops before any collector request and directs the operator to a complete free refresh. Same-day use is tested; cross-day support is deliberately not claimed. No carried review/fetch clock is rewritten to make that path pass.
9. **Bounded publication.** The initial pure migration changes only ARR, VC and enterprise cards, recomputes dependent Bubble summary/metadata, appends r6 history and retains the other 24 cards byte-equivalently at object level. It records original generation time and snapshot digest, and makes zero requests. Current and previous editorial layers must be absent at this migration boundary; no old text is rebound. The separate editorial revision waits for the final r6 data.

## Verification and release

Required: targeted date, source-identity, coherent-forgery, day-45/46 and day-120/121 tests; all r1–r5 regressions; exact arithmetic and compact editorial replay; same-day four-source success and next-day zero-call hold; original 24-card and history equality; full `check:changed` / `check:all`, measured unit suite, independent actual-diff review, exact-head CI and both-site production acceptance. The publication fixture must decompress to the stated source snapshot digest.

No provider or search request, new dependency, external credential, workflow dispatch or paid source is needed for this migration. Serial integration must preserve the separate news hygiene work. A rollback is a reviewed revert of the isolated r6 change with its matching configuration/data epoch, not selective re-labeling of r6 values as r5.
