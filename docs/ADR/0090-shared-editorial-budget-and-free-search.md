# ADR-0090: Shared CNY editorial cap and verified free-search admission

## Status

Owner-authorized implementation and publication through push/PR, independent AI review, serial merge and deployment after required checks. The owner also approved the shared CNY 5 monthly DeepSeek cap. Actual provider generation remains conditional on verified budget/account evidence and the runtime gates below; this ADR itself is not proof that activation prerequisites, ledger initialization or production acceptance have completed. This phase has performed no remote configuration, ledger initialization, provider request or production refresh. Actual verification belongs in the [project backlog](../PROJECT_BACKLOG.md).

## Context and scope

The owner approved a **CNY 5 per month total for the two article modules together**, Macro Risk and Bubble Watch weekly editorial. It is not CNY 5 per module or per key. Tavily and Brave were confirmed to be free accounts, but their remaining allowances were not established. Free-account status alone is not evidence of remaining quota. Unknown history, billing policy, account identity or allowance must stop requests.

Daily, main scoring, article facts, quality/review/attribution/freshness gates and unrelated Wind indicators remain unchanged. Only the CEO-news paid Wind fallback is retired. Existing deterministic fallbacks remain available. A budget hold means no new article; it must not be described as successful generation.

## Decision: DeepSeek admission

- `scripts/lib/deepseek-budget.mjs` is the shared runtime authority. Both article entry points default to this gate, including direct/manual calls. The allowed consumers are exactly `macro-editorial` and `bubble-editorial`; `manual-external-ai` is not an additional consumer funded by this approval. Missing configuration cannot bypass the gate.
- The policy is CNY only, with exact request model `deepseek-v4-flash`, exact response alias `deepseek-flash`, disabled thinking, JSON output and 8,000 maximum output tokens. The documented version is `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017`, but the request alias does **not** pin the underlying vendor version. A checked model string alone is not a promise of immutable pricing or model behavior.
- The recorded [official price contract](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/quick_start/pricing/) uses CNY 2 per million uncached input tokens and CNY 8 per million output tokens. Admission reserves the entire 1,048,576-token context at the uncached rate, plus all 8,000 output tokens, deliberately double-counting that output against context: **CNY 2.161152 per attempt**. This is a worst-case reservation, not average cost. Even with zero historical debit, only **two attempts** fit within CNY 5; a third requires CNY 6.483456 and is refused. Historical debits can reduce that number further. Lower usage never automatically buys another attempt.
- A reviewed price/policy digest is pinned separately and expires within 24 hours, with a 76-second safety margin before expiry. Changed/unknown currency, price review, request parameters, response model, usage, or balance shape means hold. Runtime admission checks the official CNY balance, but a balance is neither historical-spend evidence nor a reservation. No automatic FX conversion or model upgrade exists.
- The fixed `deepseek-usage-ledger` branch holds an append-only audit chain with a pinned genesis SHA. GitHub commits parent the exact observed head and update the ref without force; DeepSeek does not retry even a confirmed metadata CAS conflict; a later invocation must reassess current history. Uncertain writes, missing/corrupt state, unexpected ancestry and history replacement stop admission. Every GitHub metadata response, each body read/completion and the final pre-dispatch check must retain bounded clock agreement with the observed server Date; a long pause or clock step holds. If a ref update has already succeeded but the response or pre-dispatch clock check fails, send zero paid requests and keep the reservation pending for later invocations. No automatic initialization, month reset, history deletion, refund or retry of a generation request exists.
- Reserve before every paid POST and record a bounded outcome afterward. Valid completed output still retains the **full reserve** for 31 days after completion, plus a 152-second cross-process clock margin: twice (60-second accepted clock skew + 15-second metadata deadline + 1-second HTTP Date precision). Boundary equality remains charged. Unknown timeout, crash, missing receipt or network outcome never ages away and blocks further admission. A reviewed provider billing record can close an uncertain liability, but still does not refund the full reserve. A contract violation remains a durable hold.
- Workflow preflight reads the ledger before any article search discovery. It is only an eligibility snapshot, not a paid reservation; the provider gate must recheck and atomically reserve immediately before the call. Budget credentials are step-local to this preflight and the paid step, and the provider key exists only in the paid step. Existing upstream/day/input/recheck guards still apply.

### One-time bootstrap, never a calendar reset

Every route requires an evidence-bound cutover, CNY identity, no outstanding requests and disabled ungated callers. No route may overwrite a pre-existing ledger.

1. **Unknown complete history:** `full-cap-quarantine` debits the full CNY 5 for 31 days plus the clock margin from cutover. This does not claim previous spending was within the cap.
2. **Complete reviewed request history:** `reviewed-complete-history` requires all keys and both article consumers, records covering at least the entire 31-day-plus-margin lookback, and an independently approved canonical bootstrap digest. Historical debit equals the sum of complete records. Empty history is permitted only with positive evidence that all project history is zero.
3. **Verified current calendar month:** `reviewed-current-calendar-month` may use the complete current-month **account-all-keys total as an upper bound**. Evidence must bind the dashboard, account and key, prove the actual fixed **billing** timezone/UTC offset and complete month-start coverage, and establish a bounded 5-minute reporting lag with separate `lagBoundEvidenceSha256` proof. A notice saying reporting “may be delayed” is not a verified upper bound. A displayed timezone alone does not prove the billing timezone, and a guessed lag is not acceptable. Every prior request must be drained and ungated callers disabled before waiting the lag plus 152 seconds. Observation and cutover must be in the same billing month, no more than 5 minutes apart; offline preparation must also be within 5 minutes of the observation. Observation, cutover and preparation must each remain in that billing month under both ±76-second clock extremes. Add one full CNY 0.01 display quantum to the displayed spend, rather than rounding down. For example, a verified displayed CNY 0.14 becomes a CNY 0.15 debit, not zero. All evidence digests and timestamps require independent approval. This single genesis migration retains its debit for 31 days plus margin and does not recreate the ledger next month.

If any of those facts is missing, current-month bootstrap remains unavailable. A low balance/spend screenshot or elapsed calendar boundary does not silently unlock requests.

## Decision: shared free-search controls

The complete search endpoint inventory is four callers: Macro article discovery, Bubble article discovery, Oil News diagnosis/build and Bubble CEO search (including its source-audit caller). Local/manual/Actions requests share the same durable authorities, not separate process counters or per-key budgets. No new source, cadence increase or provider retry is introduced.

- **Tavily:** retain ADR-0059's 950-credit project budget over 31 days **counted by recorded reservation timestamps**, split 800 automated / 150 manual, plus 50 credits of account headroom. Official `/usage` must confirm Researcher/free plan, no PAYGO capacity, valid account/key allowance and enough remaining quota before a one-credit basic search is reserved. Existing unknown usage or ledger state remains hold.
- Every `/usage` probe, including explicit operator probes, is itself reserved through `tavily-meter-ledger`: at most 10 attempts per 10 minutes across keys and processes. Conservative implementation retention is 661 seconds: 600 seconds plus 30-second dispatch interval, 30-second accepted runner/server skew and 1-second HTTP precision. This is an implementation safety margin, not a claimed change to Tavily's published limit. GitHub HTTP Date is required and checked; unavailable/untrusted time holds. Failures count and delayed dispatch is refused; a stale cached meter is not an eligibility substitute.
- The existing Tavily status workflow and `review:tavily-budget -- --verify` now read ledger state only. They do **not** call provider `/usage` or reserve meter capacity. Explicit `--probe` / `--usage-only` are separate metered operations, not part of a read-only status check. A missing meter requires separately authorized initialization, never silent creation. Genesis `initializedAt` is taken from the authoritative GitHub 404 response Date. The first 661 seconds, including equality, are held to cover unknown pre-initialization probes; with whole-second timestamps the first safe admission is at 662 seconds. Missing/legacy genesis is not automatically migrated.
- **Brave:** no official monetary-balance API is assumed. `brave-usage-ledger` requires a verified dashboard grant containing the actual request limit, existing usage, expiry, zero-charge confirmation, evidence reference and fingerprint of an exclusive repository key. At most 950 remaining verified requests and a 31-day grant are permitted, with a separate 950-request/31-day project counter based on recorded reservation timestamps. This is a request allowance, not money or a fabricated free-tier entitlement. Unknown quota, absent/expired grant, unexpected key or exhausted allowance means hold. A new month or key rotation does not automatically renew it.
- Brave reserves one request by CAS before transport. Timeouts, aborts, network loss, 5xx, invalid JSON and unknown/error bodies leave permanent unresolved debt. A later dashboard grant cannot erase it. Only a structurally proven actual HTTP 4xx rejection may be terminal failed; it still consumes the reservation. 402/429/432/433 also create a durable quota hold. No automated settlement/refund route is provided.
- Tavily and Brave summaries and `--plan` explicitly report `countBasis: recorded_reservation_time` and `strictDispatchWindowVerified: false`. These project counters are **not independently verified real-dispatch rolling-window guarantees**: legacy Tavily history lacks trusted dispatch/completion timing. Preserve every existing ledger record, schema and quota; do not invent past proof, migrate timestamps or reset history. The actual free-only admission remains verified Tavily Researcher/PAYGO-disabled status plus current account/key usage and headroom, and Brave's verified exclusive-key free grant plus unresolved-debt holds. This limitation does not weaken the independently clock-verified Tavily `/usage` meter above.
- New search admissions have a 30-second monotonic dispatch deadline for both providers. Brave additionally requires the successful CAS input's trusted GitHub `serverTime` plus that entire 30-second admission bound plus 1-second HTTP Date precision to be strictly before `grant.expiresAt`. A slow runner at the true expiry therefore sends zero searches; a near-expiry grant can hold early. This forward-looking bound does not retroactively prove legacy dispatch times.
- Search budget errors stop the relevant key pool rather than rotating around the hold. Search-health failures remain failures, not healthy zero-credible-news skips. Missing searches cannot relax article reference, evidence or quality rules.

## Explicit, narrow supersession of existing assertions

1. `scripts/check-bubble-watch.mjs` previously required the literal `capSingleSourceCeoHedgingRed`, a helper used only by the retired CEO Wind path. Replace **only that helper-presence assertion** with assertions on the unchanged active merge path: two independent confirming sources are required for red, and a strongest red result with fewer sources is capped at yellow. Keep `redRequiresTwoIndependentNewsSources` and add an explicit ban on `fetchCeoHedgingFromWindNews`. This preserves the fact/quality rule and strengthens the no-paid-fallback boundary; it does not authorize weakening the red threshold or dropping the test.
2. ADR-0059's existing Tavily ledger, history, schema and numeric quotas remain in force. Its 31-day counter is now explicitly described as reservation-time accounting, not proof of a strict real-dispatch window. Its assumptions that Brave remains outside runtime quota admission, and that the read-only status workflow itself calls `/usage`, are superseded by the new Brave gate and separate metered probe. Static cadence checks remain an additional guard, not proof of current quota.
3. **No Epoch/Bubble production-job hash supersession is needed or granted.** `refresh-bubble-watch.yml` remains unchanged, including its step-local SEC contact and search ledger token. The original hashes and all candidate/read-only assertions in `tests/unit/epoch-arr-weekly.test.mjs` are preserved exactly. The source-audit workflow also remains unchanged. Do not normalize away arbitrary environment or workflow changes.

## Consequences and verification

Article availability is intentionally lower than the old schedule suggests: the worst-case cap may permit only two attempts per 31 days, and unfinished/unknown attempts can hold indefinitely. Do not weaken freshness, quality or fact gates, fabricate evidence, restore the paid CEO Wind fallback, delete old tags/ledgers or borrow another consumer's budget to improve availability.

Offline verification is registered in `check:all`: `check:deepseek-budget`, `check:free-search-budget`, `check:editorial-budget-wiring`, plus the existing Tavily/key-pool checks and unchanged editorial quality suites. Coverage includes races/uncertain writes, exact cap boundaries, clock margins, bootstrap evidence, permanent unknown outcomes, real default entry points with missing configuration, refusal of the manual third consumer, preflight ordering, secret scope and repository-wide search-call inventory. No provider call is needed for these tests. A passing fixture is not account verification, initialization or production acceptance.

Read-only/offline operator entry points and activation prerequisites are in [OPERATIONS](../OPERATIONS.md#shared-editorial-budget-and-free-search-admission). Deployment must preserve ledger history and the restrictive posture; rollback must not restore ungated requests. No dependency or package-lock change is required.
