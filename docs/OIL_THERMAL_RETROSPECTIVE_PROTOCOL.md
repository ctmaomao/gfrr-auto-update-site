# Retrospective thermal observation: frozen eligibility protocol v1

Protocol with historical archive and event-evidence replays. A neutral GOES cloud mask was inspected, but no VIIRS pixel was qualified or matched to an event. No target coverage, benchmark result or score change is claimed. Scope: delayed broad-region aggregate research, not live asset monitoring, alerts, event attribution, market direction or probabilities. No new runtime requests, credentials, workflow, budget, retention or baseline change. Existing [source restrictions](OIL_THERMAL_ANOMALY_SOURCE_REVIEW.md) and [retirement decision](ADR/0086-oil-thermal-source-retirement.md) remain in force.

## Stage 1: can the observation be assessed?

1. Before looking at outcomes, seal a manifest of broad land region, fixed spatial units and weights, completed UTC windows (including all empty windows), sampling cadence, chronological split, decision cutoff, dataset editions, and method fingerprint. Keep the full denominator; no hotspot- or news-selected sample. One preselected historical region/window can test feasibility, not skill.
2. Use the full standard 375 m L2 swath mask and algorithm QA, not the FIRMS detection-only points. [NOAA-20 fire](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/VJ114IMG) and [NOAA-21 fire](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/VJ214IMG) are Collection 2.0; [NOAA-20 IMG geolocation](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/VJ103IMG) and [NOAA-21 IMG geolocation](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/VJ203IMG) are listed as 2.1. A CMR footprint is an invitation to check, not a clear pixel. [NASA's catalog](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/viirs-jpss2-active-fires-6-min-l2-swath-375m-v002) also points to MODLL. Do not infer alignment from product names. Require platform, acquisition interval, orbit/scan identifiers, dimensions/order, lineage, geolocation QA and independent registration against published in-file fire-pixel coordinates where available. Validate the mapping on a separate calibration sample before applying to empty swaths. Reject ambiguous matches and duplicate revisions; pin collection/granule IDs, revision, checksums and processing family.
3. Decode [NASA Collection 2 guide v1.2, tables 1–2](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/2025-06/VIIRS_C2_AF-375m_User_Guide_1.2.pdf). For this conservative land-only audit reject fill, non-nominal input/geolocation QA, flagged glint or magnetic anomaly, residual bowtie and invalid geolocation. Preserve counts for unprocessed, deleted, glint, water, cloud, unclassified and low confidence separately. Among qualified land pixels, class 5 = algorithm did not report fire; 8/9 = nominal/high-confidence thermal detection. Class 7 is uncertain, not zero. Deduplicate overlapping support; calculate coverage using the frozen land area, not the number of returned pixels. Choose each window’s sensor/pass opportunity in the sealed manifest, without looking at returned masks; no best-pass substitution. For mixed masks report a signal state and a separate coverage flag: (1) any QA-qualified 8/9 on required support = detected, even if coverage is partial; (2) otherwise any unresolved class 7 on required support = uncertain, retaining the coverage flag; (3) otherwise missing or unusable required support = partial (unknown if none assessable); (4) only if every required unit has complete QA-qualified class-5 support at that opportunity = no reported detection. Never let one class-5 pixel cancel a detection, uncertainty or gap. Even complete support cannot exclude a transient event between passes.
4. Record acquisition, processing, external publication (if verifiable), and independently evidenced availability to the decision process separately, for both arms. Archive retrieval today measures retrospective visibility only. No first-availability proof by cutoff means no as-of evaluation. Do not substitute a reprocessed standard edition for an earlier NRT edition. Preserve sensor/day-night and missing reasons; same-algorithm passes share weather and surface conditions, not independent event confirmations. [NASA access](https://urs.earthdata.nasa.gov/documentation/what_do_i_need_to_know): metadata is public; DAAC downloads generally need a free Earthdata account. Check product-specific terms and citation before reuse; no download or account creation is part of this protocol.

## Stage 2: does it add evidence over the baseline?

Freeze the nonsatellite rule A, augmented rule B, ascertainment window, numerical minimum useful gain and maximum tolerated false-alert increase **before** unblinding. No numerical margins supplied means descriptive reporting only. Use identical windows, decisions, information cutoffs and event deduplication; B falls back to A when thermal data are unavailable. Independent blinded adjudication must cover positives and explicit event-free controls, not treat news silence as a negative. Separate planned, unplanned, indeterminate and out-of-scope outcomes. The [CSB 2023 Marathon Martinez renewable-fuels fire](https://www.csb.gov/marathon-martinez-renewable-fuels-fire-/) illustrates an independent outcome source only; it is not a crude-pilot event, thermal match or test result.

Hold out whole later episodes (purge overlapping windows). Primary gain = (B-only minus A-only detected adjudicated event episodes) / all independently adjudicated in-scope positive episodes; count each episode once and retain episodes with missing thermal data via fallback. Harm = 100 × (B-only minus A-only alerted adjudicated negative windows) / all adjudicated negative windows, counting at most one alert per arm per window; also report the unnetted B-only false alerts. Paired-observable gain/harm are secondary. Report eligible/total, missingness by stratum and paired counts. Freeze two-sided 95% percentile intervals using 10,000 paired cluster-bootstrap resamples, seed 20261119, before unblinding: resample whole event episodes for gain and whole predeclared nonoverlapping time blocks for negative-window harm, preserving both arms, then recompute each ratio. Prespecify block length, cluster IDs, RNG implementation and minimum independent cluster counts in the sealed manifest; inadequate clusters or undefined intervals preclude a decision. A useful result requires the gain interval to clear the frozen benefit margin and harm interval to stay below its cap. An upper gain bound below the minimum rules out that prespecified practical benefit for this cohort; excess false-alert harm separately fails acceptance. Missing clocks, inadequate controls, sparse clusters or intervals spanning the margins mean **inconclusive**. No promotion follows any result.

The [offline comparator](../tools/oil-directional/compare-thermal-holdout.mjs) implements the paired arithmetic for independently audited, sealed inputs. It reports full-cohort gain/harm, paired-observable results and missingness by planning stratum. An episode-linked purported control, missing as-of receipt or unresolved truth makes the cohort inconclusive, rather than removing that row and producing a biased estimate. Missing and malformed receipt clocks both leave the sealed window unresolved; invalid clocks defining the sealed manifest still reject the input. Reproduce the arithmetic and rejection tests with `node --test tests/unit/compare-thermal-holdout.test.mjs`. These tests use synthetic inputs. The helper does not authenticate provenance, choose event/control windows, prove block independence or enable production.

A public operational comparator is still missing at facility level: [California's maintenance review](https://www.energy.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2024-06/CEC-200-2024-010.pdf) describes refinery-specific maintenance data as confidential, and [the public forms](https://www.energy.ca.gov/files/piira-forms-and-instructions) are not observations. [EIA's dated weekly archives](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/) separate release from week-ending dates and offer PAD-district gross inputs. They are a candidate for a separate regional-throughput feasibility check, not normal-site labels or an admitted cohort here.
